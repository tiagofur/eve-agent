# Pepinillo — Product Knowledge (Creapolis.Dev)

Pepinillo is Creapolis.Dev's native **deep-work operating system**: a focus timer, lightweight task planning, and reflection analytics in one calm, native app. Its core loop is **Plan → Focus → Complete → Reflect**. It is for individual deep work — not a team or collaboration tool. The name is a playful contrast ("pickle") with the seriousness of deep work.

## Who it is for

- Knowledge workers and creatives who do deep work daily (writers, analysts, researchers, designers, developers).
- Remote workers who need daily structure and flexible focus methods.
- Students — LATAM-first: Spanish/Portuguese support and regional pricing.
- Professionals rebuilding focus habits; a strong fit for ADHD / flexible-cognition users because techniques are freely switchable.
- Watch users who want to start and control sessions from the wrist.

## The differentiator: 4 focus modes, freely switchable

| Mode | How it works | Best for | Tier |
| ---- | ------------ | -------- | ---- |
| **Pomodoro** | Classic 25/5/15 cycles, fully customizable, auto-advances ("3 of 4") | Studying, admin tasks, rigid structure | Free |
| **Flowtime** | Open-ended session; the app suggests breaks from focused time (10 min focus → 2 min break, 50 min → 10 min) | Creative work, writing, coding — no forced interruptions of flow | Paid |
| **Time Block** | Fixed block (e.g. "2 hours of writing") with continuous countdown and check-ins; integrates with Apple/Google Calendar | Time-blocking practitioners, full-day planning | Paid |
| **Stopwatch** | Counts up, no pressure or targets, tap to pause/stop | Casual tracking, unstructured days | Free |

**Mode Intelligence** (paid): after 2+ weeks of data, the app suggests which mode works best per task type, e.g. "You complete 80% more writing tasks with Flowtime."

## Plan (lightweight tasks)

Tasks with subtasks, recurrence, energy tags (deep / shallow / admin), estimated focus time per task, drag-to-prioritize with a daily focus-time total. Imports tasks from Apple Reminders (iOS) or Google Tasks (Android). Deliberately medium-depth: serious personal planning, not a project manager.

## Reflect (analytics)

- Free: today + last 7 days. Paid: 30-day + 1-year history, Weekly Review and Monthly Review with trend comparison.
- Focus streaks (consecutive days with ≥ 1 completed session) with milestones at 7/30/100/365 days; paid includes 1 free streak skip per week.
- AI Reports generated on-device with Apple Intelligence (with a deterministic template fallback, so they always work) — paid, Apple platforms.
- Focus time logs to Apple Health / Google Health Connect as Mindful Minutes.

## Native ecosystem depth

Home Screen, Lock Screen and Control Center / Quick Settings widgets; Live Activity + Dynamic Island (iOS) or persistent notification (Android); full Apple Watch and Wear OS companions (task list, start any mode, haptic alerts); Siri / Google Assistant voice starts ("Hey Siri, start a 2-hour deep work block in Pepinillo"); automatic Do Not Disturb during sessions; 4 themes + iOS 26 system glass on iOS, Material You dynamic color on Android; ambient soundscapes (rain, café, white noise) that stop during breaks.

## What Pepinillo is NOT

No team/collaboration features. No note-taking or document editing (complements Notion, Craft, Google Docs). No calendar replacement (aligns with Apple Calendar / Google Calendar). Not a meditation or wellness product — calm in tone, productivity in purpose.
