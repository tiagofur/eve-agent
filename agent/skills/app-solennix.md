# Solennix — Product Knowledge (Creapolis.Dev)

Solennix is Creapolis.Dev's **all-in-one event-management suite** for event organizers in LATAM (weddings, quinceañeras, corporate events): professional quotes and contracts (PDF), clients, products/recipes, inventory, staff and teams, calendar, payment tracking, a post-event client portal, plus inbound tools (public profile, request inbox, provider directory) in one platform. It is for organizers running their **own** business — small business owners juggling clients, quotes, inventory, payments and event-day logistics, many migrating from WhatsApp + spreadsheets. Web is the primary desktop workspace; iOS/Android for on-the-go checks and quick captures. Brand feel: elegant and professional (reference: Honeybook, Dubsado); UI in Spanish, with ES/EN support.

## Who it is for

- Event organizers in LATAM: catering, decoration, photography, music/DJ, flowers, furniture, etc.
- Solo organizers and small teams moving off WhatsApp + spreadsheets who want to look more professional (quotes, contracts, client portals).
- Organizers who also want to **get found by new clients** — public profile, request forms and the provider directory.

## The core loop: quote → confirm → execute → close

Events move through states (quoted / confirmed / completed / cancelled). Everything hangs off that cycle:

- **Events** — multi-step form (general, products, extras, equipment/staff, finances), global discount %/fixed, tax, deposit, cancellation policy, per-event currency, photo gallery, checklists, equipment conflict detection with suggestions. Unified Events + Calendar (list and calendar views, unavailable-date blocking).
- **Clients** — lightweight CRM with history (total events, total spent), quick add from an event.
- **Products & inventory** — products with recipes (ingredients/equipment), categories, images; inventory with stock levels, minimums, cost, low-stock alerts.
- **PDFs (generated server-side, consistent across platforms)** — quote, contract, shopping list, checklist, equipment list, payment report, and "quick quote" without creating a full event.
- **Payments — read this carefully:** Solennix **does not move money between the organizer and their clients.** The organizer records payments manually (balance, deposits, multiple methods). On Pro, the client can *report* a transfer through the client portal and it lands in the organizer's inbox to approve/reject. Stripe / App Store / Google Play are used **only** for the organizer's own Solennix subscription. No commissions, no fiscal invoices.
- **Staff & teams** — staff catalog, assign to events with fees, shifts with RSVP, teams that expand into events. Seats by plan (see pricing). Email notification on assignment (Pro+); collaborators get their own login portal (Business).
- **Client portal (post-sale)** — shareable per-event link where the client sees their event info and can report payments; basic on Free, full with organizer branding on Pro.
- **Inbound & growth (no marketplace)** — opt-in public profile at `/u/<slug>` (services, portfolio, packages, FAQ, reviews on Pro), shareable request links and public forms so visitors request a quote without an account, organizer inbox to accept/decline, and a public provider directory at `solennix.com/providers` searchable by city and service type. Solennix facilitates contact — **no pay-per-lead and no commission**; the deal stays between organizer and client.
- **Insight** — dashboard with KPIs, alerts (stock, payments, unconfirmed events), upcoming events; revenue chart and inbound analytics (profile views → requests) on Pro+.
- **Nice-to-haves** — mobile widgets and iOS Live Activity / Lock Screen, biometric unlock, global search, multi-currency, installable web app (PWA), self-serve account deletion.

## Pricing (as published at https://solennix.com/pricing, in MXN; verified 2026-11-10)

**Básico — Free, $0.** No credit card required to sign up. Includes: up to **4 events per month**, **20 clients**, **15 products + 30 inventory items** (combined catalog cap 20), **1 staff member**, **3 active request links**, basic client portal and basic public profile (up to 3 services), calendar, PDFs, manual payment tracking.

**Pro** — launch price **$149 MXN/month** (regular $199) or **$1,499 MXN/year** (regular $2,499):
- Unlimited events, clients, catalog and request links.
- 5 staff seats, email notification on assignment.
- Full branding on PDFs and client portal, editable contract template.
- Payment submissions (client reports a transfer → organizer inbox).
- Full public profile: portfolio, packages, FAQ, reviews; inbound analytics and standard directory listing.
- Priority support.

**Business** — **$499 MXN/month** or **$4,999 MXN/year**. Everything in Pro plus:
- Unlimited staff seats and collaborator login portal.
- **Featured placement** in the provider directory.
- 1:1 support with personalized onboarding.
- WhatsApp Business API and public API/webhooks are **announced as roadmap — never present them as already live.**

**Free trial:** 14 days on Pro and Business, cancel anytime; cancelation keeps access until the end of the paid period. **Limits never delete data:** hitting a limit only blocks new creations and shows an upgrade prompt.

Mobile purchases happen through each store's paywall — if asked for exact mobile prices, say prices are shown in the app's paywall / store listing rather than quoting numbers not on this list.

## What Solennix is NOT

- Not a marketplace and not a lead-selling scheme: no pay-per-lead, no commission on events.
- Not a payment processor for events: no online checkout for the organizer's clients, no fiscal invoicing (payments are manual records; Pro clients can only *report* a transfer).
- Not accounting software — it tracks income and balances, not tax filings.
- Not for end clients planning their own event: the tool is for organizers; end clients interact via portals/links the organizer shares. (Someone *looking for* a provider can search the public directory.)
